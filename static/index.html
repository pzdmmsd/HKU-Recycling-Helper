    HOW IT WORKS

    Clarity, with the sources
    to back it up.

    Describe one everyday item in any language, including its material and whether it is empty, clean, or stained. Typos are normalized automatically before checking the rules for HKU re Facilities.

    1. 01

      Check the scope

      Hazardous, laboratory, and commercial waste requests are excluded. Campus staff and on-site bin signage always take priority.

    2. 02

      Match cited evidence

      Live mode retrieves matching records from the local rule collection and recommends suitable campus facilities (re Kiosk, Smart Recycling Bins, or re Bins).

    3. 03

      Clear decision states

      Results show Accepted, Not accepted, Cannot determine, or General guidance when an item is outside the campus rule set.

    Sample mode provides built-in examples for common items so you can preview how decisions and citations are presented.
