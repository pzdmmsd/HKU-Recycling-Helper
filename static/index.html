    HOW IT WORKS

    Clear sorting guidance,
    backed by official sources.

    Enter one everyday item in Chinese or English. The helper checks it against HKU recycling rules and tells you how to prepare it and which campus facility to use.

    1. 01

      Safety & scope check

      Hazardous, laboratory, medical, and commercial waste cannot be placed in campus recycling bins and must follow specialist disposal procedures.

    2. 02

      Match HKU rules & facilities

      Matches your item to the appropriate stream at re Kiosk (Run Run Shaw Podium), Smart Recycling Bins, or campus re Bins.

    3. 03

      Preparation & citations

      Shows required cleaning steps (such as rinsing or removing lids) alongside links to the official HKU Photo Guide and EPD references.

    Sample mode lets you preview common examples right away; switch to Live AI to check any item of your own.

    recycling references.

    General EPD tips provide background guidance for items not covered by specific HKU rules. Always follow on-site campus bin signage.
    ABOUT THIS HELPER

    Campus recycling,
    made simple.

    HKU Recycling Helper helps students and staff check whether everyday items can be recycled at the re Kiosk (Run Run Shaw Podium, Main Campus), Smart Recycling Bins, and campus re Bins.

    Privacy

    Please enter only item descriptions and avoid personal details. No query logs or conversation histories are saved by this application.

    Facilities covered

    Covers paper, plastics, metals, glass bottles, and beverage cartons (Tetra Pak) across HKU re Facilities, with direct links to the official campus recycling map.
